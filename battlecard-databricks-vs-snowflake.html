Opportunity Threat
Who they are

A cloud data warehouse built for governed SQL analytics and data sharing across clouds.

Why they win

Teams whose primary need is standard BI/analytics dashboards, wanting the simplest, most predictable SQL-only experience with strong concurrency.

Why we win

Teams running complex ETL, data engineering, or building custom ML/AI models who want one governed platform end-to-end instead of stitching two systems together.

Questions to ask

  • How much of your roadmap involves custom ML model training or building AI agents, versus standard SQL reporting?
  • Do any of your pipelines need sub-second streaming latency?
  • How are you governing ML/AI assets today alongside your data governance?

Objection handling

Snowflake is faster for our BI dashboards.

True for standard concurrent BI queries. Worth asking whether BI is the only workload, or if ML/data engineering also needs to run somewhere — that's usually where a second platform starts adding cost and complexity.

Our analysts only know SQL, Snowflake feels simpler.

Databricks SQL is now BI-optimized (serverless, Photon) — analysts can stay SQL-only, the platform doesn't force Python or Spark on anyone who doesn't need it.

We've already invested in Snowflake's governance.

Fair, and that's real switching cost. Worth asking if that governance already extends to ML models and AI agents, or just tables — that gap is usually where teams end up bolting on a second system.
