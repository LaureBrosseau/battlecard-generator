Shopify vs. BigCommerce

Shopify Ottawa, Canada · founded 2006 · public (NYSE)
BigCommerce Austin, USA · founded 2009 · private (Apax/GIC, Nov 2024)
Advantage: Shopify Advantage: BigCommerce
Who they are

A hosted e-commerce platform built for fast, DTC-first launches, with the largest app and theme ecosystem in the category.

Why they win

Merchants scaling complex B2B or hybrid B2B/B2C operations with large, variant-heavy catalogs, or wanting to avoid Shopify's GMV-based plan structure.

Why we win

DTC-first brands who value the largest app ecosystem, fastest time-to-launch, and Shopify Payments' integrated checkout.

Questions to ask

  • Is wholesale/B2B a primary revenue stream, or a secondary add-on to DTC?
  • How many SKU variants does your largest product line need?
  • Are you using a payment gateway outside Shop Pay / Shopify Payments today?

Objection handling

BigCommerce doesn't cap our revenue the way Shopify's plan tiers do.

Worth checking their current GMV thresholds directly — they were tightened in June 2026, and orders outside their embedded gateway now carry an added fee. The "no cap" pitch isn't as clean as it used to be.

We need deep native B2B and BigCommerce has it built in.

Fair — if B2B is your primary business, that's a genuine BigCommerce strength. If it's a secondary channel alongside DTC, Shopify's B2B for All (shipped 2024) now covers most of that ground natively.

Their enterprise tier is cheaper than Shopify Plus.

On headline price, sometimes — but compare total cost of ownership, not sticker price: app costs, gateway fees, and now BigCommerce's revenue-tied fee changes all move the real number.
